Drafting Report Sheets With AI

A report sheet is your working document for a shift. It keeps track of your patients, tasks, vital signs, medications, and pending items. AI can help you organize it clearly, but you fill in the real details from your chart.

What goes on a report sheet

Typical sections:

  • Patient identifier (de-identified: “Patient A,” “Patient B”)
  • Diagnosis or reason for admission
  • Code status
  • Allergies
  • Vital signs (latest set)
  • IV access and fluids
  • Medications due this shift
  • Pending tests or procedures
  • Tasks to complete
  • Family updates or concerns

Step-by-step: draft a report sheet

  1. Open a fresh AI chat. Set the role:
“Help me draft a blank nursing report sheet template. Include sections for patient basics, vitals, IVs, medications, pending tasks, and concerns. Keep it simple and scannable. Do not include any patient names or identifying details.”
  1. Review the template. Add or remove sections to match your unit's workflow.
  2. For each patient, paste only de-identified details. Example:
Patient A: post-op day 1 hip replacement, age 70s. Vitals: BP 128/78, HR 88, temp 98.6, O2 sat 98% on room air. IV: saline lock in right hand. Meds due: acetaminophen 650 mg PRN pain, enoxaparin 40 mg subcutaneous. Pending: physical therapy eval. Pain: 4/10. Ambulated with assist x2 this morning.
  1. Ask the AI to organize the details into your report-sheet template.
  2. Review the draft against the real chart. Fix any errors. Add anything the AI missed.
  3. Copy the final version into your facility-approved system (paper flowsheet or EHR report sheet). Never save identifying details in the AI chat.

Example: de-identified report sheet draft

Patient A — Med-Surg Reason for admission: Post-op day 1, total hip replacement Code status: Full code Allergies: NKDA Vitals (0700): BP 128/78, HR 88, T 98.6, O2 sat 98% RA IV: Saline lock, right hand Meds due this shift: Acetaminophen 650 mg PO PRN pain; Enoxaparin 40 mg SQ Pending: PT evaluation Tasks: Ambulate x3 today, wound check, dressing change Pain: 4/10, last medicated 0600 Family: Spouse called, update given

This example contains no identifying information. It is safe to paste into an AI tool.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Pasting the patient's real name or room number to “help the AI remember” — do not do this
  • Asking the AI to interpret lab values or decide whether to call the provider — that is clinical judgment, not writing help
  • Copying an AI draft straight into the chart without reviewing it against the real patient
