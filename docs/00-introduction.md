# AI-Assisted Shift Documentation: A Beginner Manual for Nurses and Healthcare Workers

Welcome. This manual walks you through using AI to draft shift documentation — report sheets, handoff notes, and patient-education summaries — one step at a time, in plain English. It was written for beginners. You do not need to be technical. You do not need to know anything about artificial intelligence.

## Who this is for

- Nurses (LPN, RN, NP) and nursing students
- CNAs, aides, and other healthcare support staff
- Anyone who writes shift documentation and wants AI to make it faster and easier

## What this manual covers

1. Privacy rules first — what never to paste into an AI tool
2. Facility policy — why your employer's rules come before anything in this book
3. Getting started with an AI tool
4. Drafting report sheets with AI
5. Writing handoff notes with AI (SBAR format)
6. Creating patient-education summaries in plain language
7. A full start-to-finish shift workflow example
8. Disclaimers — education only, never medical advice

## The three rules you must remember

1. **Facility policy first.** Your employer's AI, privacy, and documentation policies override anything in this manual. Read them before you use AI at work.
2. **Never paste patient-identifying information.** No names, no medical record numbers, no dates of birth, no room numbers, nothing that could identify a real patient.
3. **Education only, never medical advice.** AI can help you draft words. It cannot assess patients, make clinical decisions, or replace your judgment. Always review every draft against the real patient chart before you use it.
