# Disclaimers: Education Only, Never Medical Advice

Read this chapter carefully. It explains what this manual can and cannot do.

## This manual is education only

Everything in this manual is for educational purposes. It explains how to use AI tools to draft words and organize information. It does not teach clinical skills, assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or medication administration.

## AI is not a clinician

AI tools:

- Cannot assess patients
- Cannot diagnose conditions
- Cannot recommend medications, doses, or treatments
- Cannot interpret lab results or vital signs for clinical decisions
- Cannot replace your nursing judgment or your facility's policies

Never ask an AI what a patient should receive, whether to call a provider, or whether a symptom is normal. Those are clinical decisions that belong to you and the care team.

## You are responsible for your documentation

AI can draft sentences, but it does not know your patient. It can miss details, mix up numbers, or sound confident while being wrong (this is sometimes called "hallucination"). You must:

- Review every AI draft against the real chart
- Confirm all medications, doses, times, and instructions with your facility-approved system
- Document only what you have personally verified

Your name goes on the documentation. You are accountable for it.

## Facility policy comes first

Your employer's policies on AI, documentation, privacy, and patient education override anything in this manual. If your facility does not allow AI-assisted drafting, do not use AI for shift documentation at work. Follow your facility's incident reporting and privacy policies if something goes wrong.

## Not a substitute for training

This manual does not replace nursing school, orientation, preceptorship, continuing education, or your facility's competency requirements. It is a writing aid, not a clinical reference.
